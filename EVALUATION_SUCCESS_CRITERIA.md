# EVALUATION SUCCESS CRITERIA

## Operator Success

An operator evaluation is successful when the operator concludes:

- accepted mutations feel safe
- refusal feels truthful
- receipts and replay provide real confidence
- the product feels meaningfully different from AI coding tools

## Team Success

A team evaluation is successful when the team concludes:

- the product reduces broken-state churn
- it lowers review friction
- it can be introduced without trust collapse
- pricing feels aligned with value and risk reduction

## Product Success Signals

Strong signals:

- repeated use after first week
- positive response to refusal discipline
- explicit trust in receipts and replay
- willingness to pay for continued use
- interest in team or enterprise rollout

## Failure Signals

Weak signals:

- expectations of broad copilot behavior
- repeated confusion about boundedness
- unwillingness to pay premium infrastructure pricing
- inability to articulate operational value
