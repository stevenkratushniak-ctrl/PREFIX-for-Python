# FOUNDING OPERATOR PROGRAM

## Program Name

Founding Operator Cohort

## Program Purpose

The first release of `PREFIX for Python` should not enter the market as a broad, low-trust utility launch.

It should enter as a controlled infrastructure evaluation for engineers and teams that care about correctness, refusal discipline, replayability, and operational trust.

The Founding Operator Cohort exists to:

- validate real operator trust under working conditions
- refine deployment and support discipline before wider scaling
- collect high-signal feedback from serious users
- establish the product as a correctness platform, not a novelty tool

## Cohort Shape

Recommended initial cohort:

- `8-15` teams
- `20-60` total engineers
- mix of individual senior engineers, small platform teams, and one or two enterprise-style evaluation accounts

This is large enough to surface operational patterns and small enough to keep support precise.

## Selection Criteria

Best early operators:

- care about correctness and review hygiene
- understand the value of refusal over guessing
- are willing to evaluate a bounded deterministic product honestly
- can provide specific workflow feedback
- do not expect copilot-style “magic”

## Operator Promise

Operators are not being asked to join an experiment.

They are being invited into a controlled release of serious correctness infrastructure with:

- a frozen canonical release bundle
- deterministic receipts and replay
- bounded product claims
- direct support and evaluation structure

## Operator Expectations

- install locally
- run the prescribed demo and evaluation flow
- use the product on bounded Python workflows
- report trust, friction, refusal clarity, and rollout concerns
- participate in scheduled check-ins during the 30-day evaluation

## Success Standard

At the end of the cohort, operators should say:

- the system behaves predictably
- refusal is understandable and valuable
- receipts and replay increase trust
- the product feels different from AI coding tools
- the rollout posture feels premium and serious
