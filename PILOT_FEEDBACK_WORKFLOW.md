# PILOT FEEDBACK WORKFLOW

## Feedback Standard

The best feedback for `PREFIX for Python` is operationally specific.

We care most about:

- trust gained or lost
- refusal clarity
- replay usefulness
- receipt comprehensibility
- rollout friction
- pricing and packaging fit

## Feedback Cadence

### Day 3

- install experience
- first-run clarity
- immediate trust reaction

### Day 10

- live workflow observations
- refusal behavior quality
- friction or confusion

### Day 21

- adoption intent
- commercial fit
- support expectations

### Day 30

- final evaluation summary
- conversion decision
- renewal or expansion path

## Feedback Template

Each report should answer:

1. What workflow was evaluated?
2. What did PREFIX accept, fix, or refuse?
3. Did the outcome increase or decrease trust?
4. Were receipts, rollback, and replay clear?
5. What would block rollout?

## Signal Ranking

Highest-value feedback:

- trust failures
- unclear refusal boundaries
- operator confusion
- deployment friction
- commercial objection rooted in category misunderstanding
