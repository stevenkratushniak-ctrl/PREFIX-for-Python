# ENTERPRISE FOLLOWUP WORKFLOW

## Purpose

Enterprise follow-up should be triggered by operational evidence, not by enthusiasm.

## Trigger Conditions

Run enterprise follow-up when one of these is true:

- conversion readiness is true
- enterprise interest is `active`
- seat count is at least `10`
- evaluation is within the final third of the 30-day term and trust level is not low

## Workflow

1. Generate `conversion-summary`
2. Review `conversion_ready`
3. Review open issues
4. Review replay/refusal/rollback milestone completion
5. Confirm canonical bundle in use
6. Move to pricing and deployment conversation

## Required Surfaces

Enterprise follow-up must stay tied to:

- `ENTERPRISE_VALUE_PROPOSITION.md`
- `BUYER_ROI_MODEL.md`
- `PRICING_AND_PACKAGING.md`

## Tone

The enterprise motion should feel:

- calm
- evidence-led
- bounded
- respectful

No aggressive upsell language belongs in this workflow.
