# Pricing Structure

## Positioning Law

`PREFIX for Python` must not be priced like a linter, editor plugin, script, or cheap AI utility.

It is a deterministic Python prefix layer for bounded correctness.

Underpricing would damage category credibility, weaken enterprise trust, and frame the product as lightweight automation instead of an operational correctness layer.

## Recommended Launch Pricing

### Community

- Free
- Individual learning and evaluation
- Local engine
- Bounded deterministic correction surface
- No team policy workflows
- No enterprise support commitments

### Pro

- Recommended launch range: `$19-$39` per developer per month
- Recommended annual range: `$199-$399` per developer per year
- Commercial professional use
- CLI and editor workflow
- Receipts, rollback, inspection, and replay
- Priority support lane

### Team

- Recommended launch range: `$49-$99` per user per month
- Shared rule catalogs
- Team policy packs
- Rollout guidance
- Shared support and admin workflows
- Release and policy coordination

### Enterprise

- Annual contract
- Recommended starting range for small teams: `$10k-$50k` per year
- Recommended range for larger organizations: `$50k-$250k+` per year
- Private deployment options
- Support and response guarantees
- Audit and replay workflows
- Custom policy packs
- Procurement-ready packaging

## Why This Commands Premium Pricing

- less debugging time burned on structurally invalid states
- fewer developer interruptions
- fewer broken commits entering review
- less review churn on obviously invalid transitions
- safer automation because ambiguity refuses instead of guessing
- stronger operator trust through receipts and replay
- lower organizational risk from hidden mutation

## Negotiation Floor

Recommended enterprise negotiation floor:

- do not position below `$10k` annually for a real team deployment
- do not collapse Pro pricing toward commodity extension pricing
- preserve the message that deterministic enforcement is infrastructure, not novelty

## Anti-Commodity Warning

Do not compare `PREFIX for Python` to:

- a cheap editor plugin
- a static linter SKU
- metered AI autocomplete

Compare it to:

- engineering hours protected
- review friction removed
- defect cost avoided
- operational trust gained
- auditability and replay capacity added
