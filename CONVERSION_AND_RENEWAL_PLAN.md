# CONVERSION AND RENEWAL PLAN

## Conversion Timing

Recommended conversion motion:

- soft commercial conversation around day `21`
- formal conversion decision at day `30`

## Conversion Paths

### Individual Operator

- convert to Pro
- annual path preferred if the operator is using PREFIX regularly

### Small Team

- convert to Team if shared workflows or policy coordination are emerging

### Enterprise Candidate

- begin enterprise follow-up in the final week of evaluation
- move into deployment, support, and procurement discussion rather than self-serve renewal

## Enterprise Follow-Up Timing

Recommended initial enterprise follow-up:

- first structured enterprise conversation between days `21-28`
- final pricing / deployment discussion in days `28-35`

## Renewal Message

The renewal frame should be:

you have now seen the trust surface in practice; the next step is whether you want this correctness boundary as part of your working environment.

## Commercial Guardrail

Do not convert with discount-heavy urgency.

That would weaken the category.

Keep the frame on:

- trust
- reduced review churn
- safer mutation
- operational confidence
