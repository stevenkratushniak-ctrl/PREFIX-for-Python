# PRICING AND PACKAGING

## Category

`PREFIX for Python` is a deterministic Python prefix layer for bounded correctness.

It is not priced as a script, linter, extension, or AI utility because the value surface is not convenience alone. The value surface is correctness control, refusal discipline, safer mutation, auditability, and interruption reduction.

## Packaging Model

### Community

- Price: free
- Audience: individual learning and bounded evaluation
- Purpose: prove the deterministic product boundary safely
- Guardrail: useful enough to evaluate, bounded enough not to undercut commercial trust

### Pro

- Price target: `$19-$39/month` or `$199-$399/year`
- Audience: individual professional developers
- Includes:
  - commercial use
  - local deterministic engine
  - VS Code embodiment
  - receipts, rollback, inspection, replay
  - professional support lane

### Team

- Price target: `$49-$99/user/month`
- Audience: engineering teams that want shared correctness policy
- Includes:
  - shared rule catalogs
  - policy packs
  - admin rollout guidance
  - team support workflows
  - upgrade coordination

### Enterprise

- Price target:
  - `$10k-$50k/year` for smaller teams
  - `$50k-$250k+/year` for larger organizations
- Audience: organizations buying correctness infrastructure, not point utility software
- Includes:
  - annual contract path
  - procurement-ready packaging
  - private deployment options
  - audit and replay workflows
  - compliance-sensitive rollout support
  - custom policy packs

## Why PREFIX Is Not Priced Like a Linter

- Linters identify issues. `PREFIX` also governs mutation boundaries.
- Linters do not usually carry replayable correction receipts.
- Linters do not usually refuse mutation as a first-class trust posture.
- Linters rarely become a correctness membrane between edit and execution.
- `PREFIX` reduces not only defects but also unsafe automation risk.

## Launch Recommendation

- Keep Community free.
- Launch Pro at the upper end of ordinary dev-tool individual pricing rather than the low end.
- Launch Team as clearly premium relative to commodity plugins.
- Keep Enterprise custom and serious from day one.

## Negotiation Guidance

- defend premium positioning with time-saved and risk-reduced economics
- avoid discounting that reframes the product as “cheap automation”
- sell the deterministic trust boundary, not “AI productivity”
