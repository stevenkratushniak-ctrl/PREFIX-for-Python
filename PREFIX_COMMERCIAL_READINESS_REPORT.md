# PREFIX COMMERCIAL READINESS REPORT

## Decision

`PREFIX for Python` is commercially ready for a premium, infrastructure-grade launch narrative.

## Recommended Launch Form

Controlled founding-operator release.

## Category Position

The product can be presented credibly as a deterministic Python prefix layer for bounded correctness.

That category remains intact if pricing stays premium and the product is not framed as a cheap extension or assistant.

## Pricing Readiness

Recommended public structure:

- Community: free
- Pro: `$19-$39/month` or `$199-$399/year`
- Team: `$49-$99/user/month`
- Enterprise: `$10k-$50k/year` starting point for smaller teams, `$50k-$250k+/year` for broader deployments

## Commercial Strengths

- differentiated category language
- refusal correctness as a trust feature
- receipts, rollback, inspection, and replay
- local-first deployment posture
- value narrative tied to debugging cost and review friction
- controlled release posture that supports premium positioning

## Commercial Warning

Do not underprice the product.

Underpricing would:

- collapse the category into commodity tooling
- weaken enterprise confidence
- make the product look like lightweight automation
- damage the credibility of the deterministic infrastructure claim

## Recommended Negotiation Floor

- Do not take real enterprise deployments below `$10k/year`
- Do not market Team as commodity plugin spend
- Keep the message tied to correctness control and operational trust

## Final Commercial Recommendation

Launch `PREFIX for Python` as a premium correctness infrastructure wedge, not as inexpensive automation.
