# ENTERPRISE VALUE PROPOSITION

## Thesis

`PREFIX for Python` gives engineering organizations a deterministic correctness membrane before execution and before review churn compounds.

## Release Model

The first enterprise path should begin through a controlled founding-operator evaluation, not through commodity self-serve positioning.

## What Enterprise Buyers Actually Care About

- fewer broken commits entering shared branches
- less time wasted on invalid intermediate states
- safer use of automated code mutation
- auditability when a tool changes source
- repeatable behavior under policy
- lower trust friction in rollout

## Enterprise Message

`PREFIX` does not ask an enterprise to trust a model's judgment.

It asks an enterprise to adopt a bounded deterministic correction layer with explicit refusal semantics, replayable receipts, and local-first operation.

## Why Audit and Replay Matter

Enterprise buyers care about:

- what changed
- why it changed
- whether the change can be replayed
- whether the tool behaves identically under the same inputs
- whether rollback is provable

That is why receipts, rollback, inspection, and replay are commercial features, not side details.

## Value Narrative

The product reduces:

- debugging waste
- review churn
- onboarding drag
- unsafe mutation risk
- hesitation around automation adoption

The product increases:

- confidence
- explainability
- policy control
- operational trust

## Enterprise Contract Range

- smaller teams: `$10k-$50k/year`
- broader deployments: `$50k-$250k+/year`

The right frame is infrastructure procurement, not plugin shopping.
